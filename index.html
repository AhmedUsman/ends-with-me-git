It EndsWith Me.

Turning Pain Into Purpose. Injustice Into Action. Advocacy Into Change.

I am Tatiana Grant — a mother, entrepreneur, advocate, and survivor of domestic violence.

01 / The story

My journey through domestic violence was not only one of physical and emotional abuse, but also a difficult fight for justice. Despite multiple criminal convictions against my abuser, I witnessed firsthand how gaps in our legal system can allow repeat offenders to receive continued leniency, leaving survivors vulnerable and accountability compromised.

I made a decision:

The cycle of abuse, injustice, and silence ends with me.

02 / The legislation

Michigan House Bill 5174

Rather than allowing my experience to define me, I chose to use it to advocate for meaningful legislative change.

That commitment led to my advocacy for Michigan House Bill 5174, legislation designed to prevent individuals who have previously received deferred judgments for domestic or interpersonal violence from repeatedly benefiting from delayed sentencing and certain dismissal opportunities.

But this mission is bigger than one bill or one survivor.

03 / The mission

A mission beyond one story.

EndsWithMe.com is a platform dedicated to empowering survivors, connecting individuals with domestic violence resources in their states, raising awareness, and advocating for stronger laws that prioritize survivor safety and offender accountability.

My hope is that through education, advocacy, and collective action, we can create a justice system that protects survivors, holds repeat offenders accountable, and helps break generational cycles of abuse.

Because surviving is only part of my story. Changing what happens next is my purpose.
